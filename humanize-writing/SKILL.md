# Humanize Writing

Rewrite for an attentive human reader, not for a detector score.

## Approach

- Preserve the writer's claims, intent, factual content, and level of confidence. Do not invent experience, results, citations, personal anecdotes, or opinions.
- Infer the audience and purpose from context. Ask a short clarifying question only when a missing choice would materially change the result.
- If the user supplies writing samples, match their stable traits—directness, vocabulary, rhythm, formality, and use of contractions—without copying distinctive phrases.
- Replace generic openings and abstract claims with clear, specific language. Prefer concrete verbs and relevant details already present in the source.
- Vary sentence and paragraph length when it improves rhythm. Use transitions that reflect the actual relationship between ideas rather than formulaic connectors.
- Remove repetition, inflated wording, canned enthusiasm, unnecessary headings, and excessive bullet lists.
- Keep technical terminology when the intended reader needs it. Explain or simplify it when the audience is nontechnical.
- Retain small signs of an individual voice, but do not add deliberate mistakes, fake uncertainty, random slang, or contrived quirks.
- Preserve formatting requirements, length limits, keywords, citations, and platform constraints supplied by the user.

## Deliverable

Return the rewritten text directly unless the user asks for alternatives or an explanation. When useful, offer no more than two distinct versions with clear differences in tone.

For professional profiles and proposals, lead with the client's problem or desired outcome, support it with truthful evidence, and end with a low-pressure next step. Avoid long technology inventories unless search keywords or job fit require them.

For academic or factual writing, preserve citations and qualifications exactly. Improve clarity and flow without disguising authorship or facilitating academic misconduct.

Never claim that a rewrite is undetectable, human-authored, or guaranteed to bypass an AI detector. If asked for evasion, redirect to improving authenticity, specificity, accuracy, and readability.
